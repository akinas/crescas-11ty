    <p class="body-text">
      <strong>Daniel Levin</strong> is a graduate of yeshivot and rabbinical academies. For years he served as a senior lecturer in Jewish studies in Moscow and has published numerous scholarly articles in Torah and academic journals.
    </p>
    <p class="body-text">
      His research focuses on the philosophy of Rabbi Hasdai Crescas, the political philosophy of Don Isaac Abravanel, the relationship between Halakha and Kabbalah, and medieval Jewish thought. He published a monograph on Abravanel's Torah commentary and contributed as translator and editor to major series on Jewish holidays. He currently works in the publications department of a research institute in Jerusalem.
    </p>
