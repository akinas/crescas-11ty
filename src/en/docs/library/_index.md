<p class="lede">Rabbi Hasdai Crescas’s Library: An exploration of medieval Jewish philosophical thought.</p>

<p class="body-text">
  This series presents a curated selection of seminal philosophical texts cited and discussed in the writings of Rabbi Hasdai Crescas. It is designed for scholars, students, and readers wishing to deepen their understanding of Crescas's thought and the intellectual movements of his era.
